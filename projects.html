BIGE: Biomechanics-informed GenAI for Exercise Science

BIGE is a clinician-guided framework for generative motion modeling that combines biomechanically meaningful scoring with a differentiable surrogate model of muscle activation to optimize latent samples under constraints such as pelvic tilt, angular velocity, center-of-mass dynamics, and vastus medialis activation ranges; on squat data, this targeted guidance produces more realistic, diverse, and physiologically valid motions than prior baselines, including deeper squats with fewer artifacts and improved temporal segmentation using joint angular-velocity peaks.
