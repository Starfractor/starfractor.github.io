Teaching Approach

Teaching is something I am deeply passionate about. As a first-generation college student, I know how impactful the right opportunities and guidance can be. I firmly believe that anyone with the right mindset and habits can achieve their goals, and I see that potential in every student I work with—regardless of skill level or background.

Most of the topics I teach are complex and rigorous, so my approach emphasizes breaking down difficult concepts into simple, digestible steps. This helps students build a strong foundation while developing the confidence to tackle subjects that can often feel overwhelming. I have experience with both one-on-one sessions and small-group lessons (up to 10 students), and I tailor my methods to fit each individual's learning style and pace.
